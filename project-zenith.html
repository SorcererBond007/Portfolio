Primary onboard flight computer handling barometric/IMU flight state tracking, pyro deployment logic, and real-time packet transmission over the 915 MHz ISM band.

Avionics Integration & RF Validation

01.Avionics Bay Layout & SolidWorks Modeling

Modeled the internal avionics bay assembly in SolidWorks to organize flight computers, power sources, and wiring harnesses within tight airframe constraints while maintaining clean mechanical access for pre-launch arming and inspection.

02.RF Interference Mitigation & Antenna Reconfiguration

Diagnosed severe RF signal attenuation and packet timeouts caused by proximity between the transmitting antenna and metallic structural airframe rods. Reconfigured internal electronics and antenna orientation to establish clean RF clearance, eliminating packet loss and ground station timeouts.

03.4,800 ft End-to-End Telemetry Validation

Validated end-to-end wireless telemetry between the airborne Silicdyne Fluctus flight computer and the bundled SteadyBlue ground station. Conducted field range testing tracking RSSI and link stability across a 4,800 ft operational envelope, reducing end-to-end telemetry latency by approximately 100 ms.
